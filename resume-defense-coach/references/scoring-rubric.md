# Scoring Rubric

This file defines the 1–5 anchored scoring system. Scores must be justified with evidence from the user's answers. Do not score by overall impression alone.

---

## Scoring Dimensions

### 1. 项目动机与问题理解 (Project Motivation & Problem Understanding)

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | Cannot clearly state the problem the project solves. Confuses "what was done" with "why it was done." |
| 3 | Can state the problem and basic motivation, but lacks depth on why the problem matters or to whom. |
| 5 | Clearly articulates the problem, its significance, target users/scenarios, and how the project addresses a real gap. Can discuss alternative problem framings. |

---

### 2. 个人贡献与项目所有权 (Personal Contribution & Ownership)

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | Describes all work as "we." Cannot isolate any personal contribution. Cannot explain any technical detail of claimed work. |
| 3 | Can identify their module or role, but details are vague. Some parts still attributed to team without clarity. |
| 5 | Clearly distinguishes personal work from team work. Can explain own design decisions, implementation details, and lessons learned from personal contributions. |

---

### 3. 技术准确性 (Technical Accuracy)

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | Major conceptual errors. Confuses fundamental concepts (e.g., classification vs regression, precision vs recall). Technical claims are incorrect. |
| 3 | Generally accurate at a surface level. May have minor imprecision or cannot explain concepts beyond a basic definition. |
| 5 | Technically precise. Can explain concepts at mechanism level with correct terminology. Connects concepts accurately to project usage. Acknowledges nuance. |

---

### 4. 方法选择与取舍 (Method Selection & Trade-offs)

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | Cannot justify any method choice. No awareness of alternatives. |
| 3 | Can state the main reason for the core method. Mentions 1–2 alternatives but cannot compare trade-offs in detail. |
| 5 | Articulates clear rationale for method choices with specific trade-offs. Compares multiple alternatives on concrete dimensions (performance, cost, complexity). Can explain what would change if constraints changed. |

---

### 5. 实验、指标与证据意识 (Experiments, Metrics & Evidence)

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | Claims results with no metrics, baselines, or evidence. Cannot define the metric used. Data and evaluation setup are completely unclear. |
| 3 | Names metrics and baselines. May have gaps in evidence (e.g., no statistical test, unclear dataset). Can describe the experiment setup at a high level. |
| 5 | Precise metric definitions. Clear baseline comparison with justification. Understands metric limitations. Can discuss experiment design choices, statistical validity, and error analysis. |

---

### 6. 对限制和失败的认识 (Awareness of Limitations & Failures)

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | Claims the project has no limitations or failures. Cannot name a single thing that didn't work or could be improved. |
| 3 | Can name 1–2 limitations at a high level, but cannot discuss specific failure cases or their root causes. |
| 5 | Honestly identifies specific limitations and failure modes. Can discuss root causes, attempted mitigations, and what they'd do differently. Understands the boundary of the approach. |

---

### 7. 逻辑结构 (Logical Structure)

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | Answers are disorganized or rambling. Cannot follow a thread of reasoning. Jumps between unrelated points. |
| 3 | Generally organized. Follows a basic structure (e.g., background → action → result), but may be incomplete or unbalanced. |
| 5 | Clear, logical structure. Uses effective frameworks (背景—任务—行动—结果—反思). Transitions naturally between points. Adapts structure to the question. |

---

### 8. 表达清晰度 (Clarity of Expression)

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | Answers are difficult to follow. Overuses jargon without explanation. Sentences are fragmented or too dense. |
| 3 | Generally clear. Some jargon may be undefined. Occasionally verbose or too terse. |
| 5 | Concise and precise. Technical terms are used correctly and explained when necessary. Complex ideas are communicated in an accessible way. Adapts level of detail to the question. |

---

### 9. 追问应对能力 (Follow-Up Response)

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | Cannot handle any follow-up. Repeats the same answer or goes silent. Becomes defensive or flustered. |
| 3 | Can handle basic follow-ups. May need to pause or think. Sometimes provides more detail on follow-up, sometimes repeats. |
| 5 | Handles follow-ups smoothly. Follow-up answers add depth beyond the original. Can adjust thinking when challenged. Acknowledges uncertainty honestly. |

---

### 10. 领域理解与未来思考 (Domain Understanding & Future Thinking)

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | No awareness of related work or field trends. Cannot articulate any future direction beyond "improve performance." |
| 3 | Can name some related work and trends. Has basic ideas for future improvement but lacks prioritization or specificity. |
| 5 | Well-informed about related work and field trends. Can position own work in the broader landscape. Has concrete, prioritized future plans grounded in current limitations. |

---

## Scoring Principles

### Evidence requirement
Every score must be accompanied by a brief justification referencing specific answers from the interview. Do not score based on overall feeling.

### Uncovered dimensions
If a dimension was not sufficiently probed during the interview (e.g., time ran out before reaching it), mark it as **"未充分评估"** instead of assigning a forced score. Do not guess.

### Scoring interactions
- **Conceptual errors** drag down Technical Accuracy (dimension 3).
- **Fabricated or unexplainable data** severely impacts Evidence awareness (dimension 5).
- **Inability to separate personal work** caps Personal Ownership (dimension 2) at 2.
- **Fluent but empty answers** — Clarity (8) can be high while all other dimensions are low. Fluency ≠ competence.
- **Pressure handling** — poor performance under follow-up should be reflected in dimension 9, not across all dimensions.

### Score interpretation

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Excellent — accurate, specific, evidence-backed, reflective. Ready for real interview. |
| 4 | Good — mostly correct with minor gaps. Close to ready. |
| 3 | Adequate — basically correct but surface-level. Needs more depth and evidence. |
| 2 | Weak — significant gaps, vague, or imprecise. Needs substantial preparation. |
| 1 | Severely lacking — major errors or inability to answer. Needs fundamental review. |

---

## Overall Assessment

After scoring individual dimensions, provide:

### Three strongest capabilities
Be specific — reference particular answers or exchanges that demonstrate strength.

### Three most serious problems
Prioritize by impact on interview performance. Focus on fixable issues with concrete improvement suggestions.

### Overall readiness
A holistic judgment on whether the user is ready for their target interview, with a brief rationale.
