## Formatting Rules for the Review

1. **优化示范回答** must only use facts from user materials and user answers during the interview.
2. When a fact is missing, use placeholders:
   - `[请补充真实的样本数量]`
   - `[请补充实际使用的评测指标]`
   - `[请明确你本人负责的模块]`
   - `[请核实该提升比例]`
   - `[项目材料中未提供这一信息]`
3. Never invent facts to make an answer look complete.
4. For concept corrections, distinguish:
   - **明确错误** — the user said something factually wrong.
   - **表述不严谨** — the idea is roughly right but the wording is imprecise.
   - **信息不足，无法判断** — we don't have enough detail to assess.
   - **可能正确，但需要项目材料支持** — the claim is plausible but unverified.
5. Review tone: constructive and coaching-oriented, not judgmental. Focus on what to improve and how.
6. Prioritize high-impact feedback. Do not spend equal time on minor wording issues and major conceptual errors.
